Cloud Operations and Management

MSc Enterprise IT Management · July 2026

This e-portfolio documents my learning and professional development throughout the Cloud Operations and Management module. It brings together practical activities, technical implementations, academic analysis, and critical reflection to demonstrate the progressive development of my knowledge and skills in modern cloud technologies.

Module Overview

The module explores the principles, technologies, and operational practices involved in designing, implementing, managing, and evaluating modern cloud environments. It combines theoretical knowledge with practical experience across cloud architecture, automation, cloud-native technologies, Microsoft Azure, hybrid and edge computing, security, disaster recovery, migration, serverless computing, artificial intelligence, and emerging cloud trends.

Throughout the module, emphasis is placed on both technical implementation and critical evaluation. The activities therefore provide opportunities to develop practical cloud skills while considering scalability, resilience, security, compliance, automation, sustainability, and organisational requirements.

Module Aims

The module aims to develop a broad understanding of cloud computing architecture, frameworks, services, and technologies while providing opportunities for practical implementation and professional reflection. Particular emphasis is placed on:

  • Understanding cloud architecture, frameworks, and the fundamental principles of cloud services.
  • Developing knowledge of cloud technologies including hybrid, edge, and fog computing, supported by practical implementation experience.
  • Reflecting on personal development and the ethical, social, and professional implications of cloud technologies.
  • Recognising current challenges, limitations, opportunities, and future developments within cloud computing.
  • Developing the ability to communicate technical arguments and recommendations to different audiences.
  • Considering the wider impact of cloud technologies on equality, diversity, inclusion, and professional practice.

Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the module, the learning outcomes require the ability to:

LO1 — Cloud Computing Fundamentals
Identify and understand the fundamental concepts that underpin cloud computing.
LO2 — Cloud Solutions
Identify and understand different cloud solutions and deployment approaches.
LO3 — Cloud Services and Tools
Identify, configure, and implement different levels of cloud services and associated tools.
LO4 — Resilient Cloud Solutions
Critically determine appropriate methodologies, tools, and techniques for developing and implementing resilient cloud solutions.
LO5 — Standards and Procedures
Articulate and identify relevant standards and procedures used when implementing cloud computing services and infrastructure.
