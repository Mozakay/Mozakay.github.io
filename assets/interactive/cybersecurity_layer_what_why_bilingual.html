Cybersecurity layer — what it protects, the attacks it prevents, and why each control exists

طبقة 1 — أمان بيانات الاعتماد والهوية security_layer.py +
ماذا تحمي؟
نقطة الدخول إلى النظام كاملاً — قبل أن تصل يد المستخدم إلى أي عملية بنكية، يجب التحقق من هويته. هذه الطبقة تحمي BankAccount.deposit() وwithdraw() وget_balance() من أي وصول غير موثوق.
الهجمات التي تصدّها
Brute Force: تجربة كلمات مرور عشوائية بالآلاف حتى تنجح واحدة.
Credential Stuffing: استخدام قوائم كلمات مرور مسرّبة من مواقع أخرى.
Timing Attack: قياس وقت الاستجابة لمعرفة ما إذا كانت كلمة المرور قريبة من الصحيح.
Session Hijacking: سرقة جلسة مفتوحة بعد خروج المستخدم.
الآلية الدفاعية
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 مع 100,000 تكرار + Salt عشوائي 32 بايت: يجعل كسر كلمة المرور مكلفاً جداً حتى بعد سرقة قاعدة البيانات.
hmac.compare_digest: مقارنة آمنة ثابتة الوقت — لا يمكن قياس الفارق الزمني.
LoginAttemptPolicy: قفل الحساب 30 ثانية بعد 3 محاولات فاشلة.
SessionManager: انتهاء تلقائي للجلسة بعد 300 ثانية من عدم النشاط.
الكلاسات الرئيسية
BasicPasswordPolicy PBKDF2PasswordHasher CredentialService AuthLoginService SessionManager LoginAttemptPolicy
طبقة 2 — التخويل والتحكم بالوصول RBAC security_layer.py +
ماذا تحمي؟
المصادقة وحدها لا تكفي — مستخدم مُوثَّق قد لا يملك صلاحية السحب. هذه الطبقة تحمي العمليات البنكية الحساسة بتطبيق مبدأ Least Privilege: كل مستخدم يحصل على الحد الأدنى من الصلاحيات لإتمام مهمته.

Privilege Escalation: مستخدم يحاول تنفيذ عمليات أعلى من مستواه.
Insider Threat: موظف داخلي يستغل وصوله لإجراء عمليات غير مخوّلة.
Horizontal Access: مستخدم يصل لحسابات غير ملكه.

ROLE_AUDITOR: قراءة فقط — لا يستطيع إجراء أي عملية مالية.

طبقة 3 — كشف الاحتيال وحماية التحويلات security_layer.py + secure_banking_services.py +
ماذا تحمي؟
أموال العملاء من المعاملات المشبوهة — حتى لو كان المستخدم مُوثَّقاً ومُخوَّلاً، لا يُسمح بالتحويل إذا كانت المعاملة تُثير الشك. أربع قواعد تعمل بـ Strategy Pattern وتُقيَّم قبل أن يغادر أي مبلغ الحساب المصدر.

LargeAmountRule: سحب مبلغ كبير فجأة يتجاوز الحد المُهيَّأ — يُشير لاختراق الحساب.
RapidOutgoingTransactionRule: تحويلات متسارعة في وقت قصير — نمط كلاسيكي لغسيل الأموال.
BalanceRatioRule: سحب نسبة كبيرة من الرصيد دفعة واحدة (مثلاً 90%) — مؤشر خطر عالٍ.
UnusualHourRule: معاملات في ساعات غير عادية (مثلاً 3 صباحاً) — مرتبطة بالاحتيال الإلكتروني.
الآلية الدفاعية
FraudDetectionEngine يُشغِّل جميع القواعد المُحقَنة على TransactionContext — إذا نجحت أي قاعدة، يُرفع FraudAlertError قبل تنفيذ العملية ويُسجَّل حدث FRAUD_DETECTED بمستوى CRITICAL. الرصيد لا يتغير.

طبقة 4 — تحديد المعدل ومنع الإغراق security_layer.py + secure_banking_services.py +
ماذا تحمي؟

StrictWindowRateLimiter: صارم — حد ثابت في نافذة زمنية محددة. مناسب للامتثال التنظيمي.

Layer 1 — Credential & identity security security_layer.py +
What it protects
The entry point to the entire system. Before any user interaction reaches a banking operation, identity must be established. This layer guards BankAccount.deposit(), withdraw(), and get_balance() from all unauthenticated access.
Attacks it prevents
Brute force: Attempting thousands of password combinations until one succeeds.
Credential stuffing: Replaying leaked username–password pairs from other breached services.
Timing attack: Measuring response latency to infer whether a submitted password is close to the correct one.
Session hijacking: Exploiting an open session after a user has ceased to be active.
Defence mechanism
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and a 32-byte random salt: makes offline cracking computationally prohibitive even if the database is stolen.
hmac.compare_digest: Constant-time comparison — response latency reveals nothing about correctness.
LoginAttemptPolicy: Account locked for 30 seconds after 3 consecutive failed attempts.
SessionManager: Automatic session expiry after 300 seconds of inactivity.
Principal classes
BasicPasswordPolicy PBKDF2PasswordHasher CredentialService AuthLoginService SessionManager LoginAttemptPolicy
Layer 2 — Authorisation & RBAC security_layer.py +
What it protects
Authentication alone is insufficient. An authenticated user may lack the privilege to withdraw funds. This layer enforces the principle of Least Privilege: each user is granted only the minimum permissions required to fulfil their role.
Attacks it prevents
Privilege escalation: A user attempting operations above their assigned authority level.
Insider threat: An internal actor exploiting authenticated access to perform unauthorised transactions.
Horizontal access: A user accessing accounts belonging to other customers.
Defence mechanism
ROLE_OWNER: Holds PERM_DEPOSIT, PERM_WITHDRAW, and PERM_TRANSFER.
ROLE_AUDITOR: Read-only — cannot initiate any financial operation.
AuthorizationStep verifies the permission before every operation in the pipeline.
Principal classes
AuthorizationPolicy InMemoryAccessRepository AuthorizationStep ROLE_OWNER ROLE_AUDITOR
Layer 3 — Fraud detection & transfer protection security_layer.py + secure_banking_services.py +
What it protects
Customer funds from suspicious transactions. Even when a user is both authenticated and authorised, a transfer is blocked if it triggers a fraud rule. All four rules are evaluated against a TransactionContext before any funds leave the source account.
Scenarios and attacks it prevents
LargeAmountRule: A sudden large withdrawal exceeding the configured threshold — indicative of an account takeover.
RapidOutgoingTransactionRule: Multiple outgoing transfers in rapid succession — a classic money-laundering pattern.
BalanceRatioRule: Withdrawing a disproportionate fraction of the balance in a single operation (e.g. 90%) — a high-risk signal.
UnusualHourRule: Transactions at atypical hours (e.g. 03:00) — strongly associated with automated fraud and account takeover.
Defence mechanism
FraudDetectionEngine runs all injected rules against the TransactionContext. If any rule triggers, FraudAlertError is raised before the operation executes, a FRAUD_DETECTED event at Severity.CRITICAL is recorded, and the account balance remains unchanged.
Principal classes
FraudDetectionEngine FraudCheckStep LargeAmountRule RapidOutgoingTransactionRule BalanceRatioRule UnusualHourRule TransactionMonitor
Layer 4 — Rate limiting & request flooding prevention security_layer.py + secure_banking_services.py +
What it protects
The entire system against volumetric exploitation. All banking operations are guarded against automated abuse through two interchangeable rate-limiting strategies.
Attacks it prevents
Denial of service / flooding: Overwhelming the system with thousands of requests per second to degrade availability.
Automated brute force: High-speed, large-scale credential guessing attempts.
API abuse: Exploiting endpoints with repeated calls to extract data or exhaust resources.
The two strategies
TokenBucketRateLimiter: Flexible — permits requests as long as tokens are available; replenishes gradually. Suited to production environments.
StrictWindowRateLimiter: Strict — enforces a hard ceiling within a fixed time window. Suited to regulatory compliance contexts.
Principal classes
TokenBucketRateLimiter StrictWindowRateLimiter RateLimiterFactory RateLimitStep TokenBucketConfig StrictWindowConfig
Layer 5 — Audit, alerting & security reporting All files +
What it protects
Accountability and traceability. Without a reliable audit trail, it is impossible to verify what occurred or to demonstrate regulatory compliance. This layer ensures that every security event — whether a successful operation or a denied request — is preserved and structurally immutable.
Risks it addresses
Undetected breach: An intrusion that leaves no traceable record.
Audit tampering: Modification of log records to conceal suspicious activity.
Compliance gap: Inability to produce evidence for regulatory auditors.
Defence mechanisms
SecurityEvent (frozen=True): Python itself prevents any mutation after creation — no component may alter a recorded event.
CriticalEventPublisher: Immediately notifies observers of critical events. Observer failures are themselves captured in InMemoryObserverFailureSink — no event is silently lost.
ThreatReportBuilder: Classifies events as HIGH / MODERATE / LOW RISK for operational review.
Principal classes
SecurityEventRecorder BoundedSecurityEventStore CriticalEventPublisher AlertObserver ThreatReportBuilder SystemReportAssembler
Layer 6 — Account registry integrity secure_banking_services.py +
What it protects
The structural integrity of the account registry — preventing systemic data conflicts that could misdirect transfers or corrupt account balances.
Risk addressed: Duplicate account numbers — if two accounts share the same number, a transfer may be routed to the wrong account or its balance may be overwritten.
AccountSecurityRegistry: Rejects registration of any account whose number already exists in the registry. Fails immediately with a ValueError before any audit logger is created for the duplicate account.

AccountSecurityRegistry AuditLoggerFactory
