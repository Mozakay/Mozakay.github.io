01 Login succeeds with correct password Registers a user and logs in with valid credentials. Asserts that an AuthSession is returned, the session is active, and a LOGIN / PASS event is recorded in the auth event log. Identity & credential
Classes: CredentialService, AuthLoginService, BoundedAuthEventLogger		 SRP — CredentialService validates only; SessionManager manages session only; BoundedAuthEventLogger records only.
DIP — IClock is injected, so the test controls time without coupling to datetime.now().
02 Login fails with wrong password Attempts login with an incorrect password. Asserts that AuthenticationError is raised and that the failed-attempt counter increments to 1. Identity & credential
Classes: LoginAttemptPolicy, AuthenticationError		 SRP — LoginAttemptPolicy tracks failed attempts only; it does not validate passwords or manage sessions.
OCP — The policy is configurable (max attempts, lockout duration) without modifying the class.
03 Account locks after maximum failed attempts Repeats incorrect login until the configured limit (3) is reached. Asserts that the account is locked and that a LOCKOUT event is recorded in the auth log. Identity & credential
Classes: LoginAttemptPolicy, AuthEventRecorder, BoundedAuthEventLogger		 SRP — Lockout logic resides solely in LoginAttemptPolicy; event recording resides solely in AuthEventRecorder.
ISP — BoundedAuthEventLogger exposes only get_auth_events() — no unrelated methods are imposed on its consumer.
04 Locked account rejects correct password After lockout, attempts login with the correct password. Asserts that AccountLockedError is raised even though the credentials are valid. Identity & credential
Classes: LoginAttemptPolicy, AccountLockedError		 SRP — Lockout enforcement is an independent check, prior to and separate from credential validation.
LSP — AccountLockedError and AuthenticationError are both subtypes of PermissionError and are substitutable in caller exception handling.
05 Session expires after timeout Logs in successfully, then advances the injected TestClock by 6 seconds beyond the configured 5-second timeout. Asserts that is_authenticated_session() returns False. Identity & credential
Classes: SessionManager, AuthSessionCoordinator, TestClock		 DIP — SessionManager depends on the IClock abstraction, not on datetime.now(). TestClock is a test double that satisfies the same interface.
SRP — Session expiry logic is isolated inside SessionManager; no other class is responsible for it.

Classes: AuthorizationPolicy, AuthorizationStep, ROLE_AUDITOR		 SRP — AuthorizationPolicy is solely responsible for permission enforcement; it does not authenticate or log.

Classes: AuthorizationPolicy, ROLE_AUDITOR, PERM_DEPOSIT		 OCP — A new role or permission may be added by extending the access repository, without modifying AuthorizationPolicy.

08 Owner can transfer between accounts Creates a user with ROLE_OWNER and transfers £100 from account 1001 to account 1002. Asserts that source balance reduces to £900 and destination balance increases to £600. Authorisation / RBAC + Transfer & fraud

SRP — Transfer validation logic is isolated in BasicTransferValidationPolicy; the transfer service orchestrates but does not validate.

09 Large transaction blocked by fraud rule Injects only LargeAmountRule(threshold=100.0) and attempts a withdrawal of £200. Asserts that FraudAlertError is raised and the account balance remains unchanged at £1,000. Fraud detection
Classes: FraudDetectionEngine, LargeAmountRule, FraudCheckStep		 OCP — The engine is not modified; a different rule is simply injected. This is the clearest demonstration of OCP in the test suite.
DIP — FraudDetectionEngine depends on the IFraudRule list abstraction, never on any concrete rule class.
10 Fraud alert observer notified on suspicious transaction Triggers a fraudulent transfer (£200 against a £100 threshold). Asserts that the AlertObserver receives at least one event with SecurityAction.FRAUD_DETECTED and Severity.CRITICAL. Fraud detection + Audit & alerting
Classes: CriticalEventPublisher, AlertObserver, InMemoryObserverFailureSink		 OCP — New observers (e.g. an SMS or SIEM observer) may be registered without modifying CriticalEventPublisher.
SRP — The publisher publishes; the observer stores alerts. Neither takes on the other's responsibility.
11 Unusual-hour transaction flagged Constructs a TransactionContext with hour_of_day=3 and passes it to FraudDetectionEngine injected with UnusualHourRule(7–22). Asserts that the engine returns a non-empty list of triggered rule descriptions. Fraud detection
Classes: FraudDetectionEngine, UnusualHourRule, TransactionContext		 LSP — UnusualHourRule satisfies the IFraudRule contract exactly as LargeAmountRule does — both are fully interchangeable inside the engine.
ISP — IFraudRule exposes a single check(context) method; implementing classes are not forced to implement any unrelated method.
12 Balance ratio exceeded blocks transfer Injects BalanceRatioRule(max_ratio=0.5) and attempts to transfer £900 from an account holding £1,000. Asserts that FraudAlertError is raised and both account balances remain unchanged. Fraud detection
Classes: BalanceRatioRule, FraudDetectionEngine, SecureTransferService		 OCP — The ratio threshold is configurable at construction; the rule class itself requires no modification.
LSP — BalanceRatioRule is a third distinct implementation of IFraudRule, substitutable without any impact on the engine or pipeline.

13 Audit event recorded on failed access Creates a user with no granted permissions and attempts a deposit. Asserts that an ACCESS_DENIED event with EventStatus.DENIED and Severity.WARNING is present in the account's security log. Audit & alerting
Classes: AuthorizationStep, SecurityEventRecorder, AuditedAccountService		 SRP — AuthorizationStep enforces permissions; SecurityEventRecorder records outcomes. Neither does the other's job.
ISP — IAccountAuditLogger exposes only get_events() and the relevant write methods — no bloated interface.
14 Successful deposit produces PASS audit event Performs a successful £50 deposit. Asserts that a SecurityAction.DEPOSIT event with EventStatus.PASS and Severity.INFO is written to the account logger. Audit & alerting
Classes: AuditedAccountService, SecurityEventRecorder		 SRP — AuditedAccountService is a decorator that adds logging to the account service without altering its domain logic — a textbook application of SRP via the Decorator pattern.
OCP — Logging behaviour is added by wrapping, not by modifying the underlying account service.
15 Rate limit exceeded produces WARNING audit event Configures a strict window of 1 request per 60 seconds, performs one deposit, then immediately attempts a second. Asserts that RateLimitExceededError is raised and a RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED event with Severity.WARNING is written to the log. Rate limiting + Audit & alerting
Classes: StrictWindowRateLimiter, RateLimitStep, SecurityEventRecorder		 LSP — StrictWindowRateLimiter satisfies the IRateLimiter contract in the same manner as TokenBucketRateLimiter.
SRP — Rate enforcement and audit recording are distinct responsibilities held by distinct classes.
16 Threat report builder summarises events Constructs a set of SecurityEvent objects manually and passes them to ThreatReportBuilder. Asserts that the resulting ThreatReport contains correct event counts and a valid risk-level summary. Audit & reporting
Classes: ThreatReportBuilder, ThreatReport, SecurityEvent, EventAmount		 SRP — ThreatReportBuilder is solely responsible for aggregating events into a report.
OCP — The immutable SecurityEvent frozen dataclass guarantees that no downstream component can alter recorded events — the audit trail is structurally closed to modification.
17 System report assembler merges security and authentication events Constructs a set of account security events and a lockout AuthEvent. Passes both to SystemReportAssembler. Asserts that the merged ThreatReport reflects total event counts from both sources. Audit & reporting
Classes: SystemReportAssembler, AuthEventToSecurityEventMapper, AuthAction.LOCKOUT		 SRP — AuthEventToSecurityEventMapper (Adapter pattern) is solely responsible for translating auth events; SystemReportAssembler is solely responsible for merging.
DIP — The assembler depends on the IThreatReportGenerator abstraction, not on a concrete generator.
18 Threat report generator builds report from logger Performs a successful deposit, then calls ThreatReportGenerator directly from the account's logger. Asserts that a valid ThreatReport is produced from the events already recorded. Audit & reporting
Classes: ThreatReportGenerator, AuditedAccountService		 DIP — ThreatReportGenerator depends on the IAccountAuditLogger abstraction; any conforming logger may be supplied.
ISP — IThreatReportGenerator exposes only generate(); no unrelated methods are imposed.
