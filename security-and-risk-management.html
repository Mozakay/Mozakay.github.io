Security and Risk Management

This page includes the posts, activities, and reflections related to the Security and Risk Management module.

Module Summary

This module focused on identifying, analysing, and managing security risks within business and digital environments. It covered qualitative and quantitative risk assessment, risk modelling, cybersecurity controls, business continuity, and disaster recovery planning.

What I Learned

  • How to identify security risks and connect them to business impact.
  • How to apply quantitative methods such as Monte Carlo simulation and Bayesian analysis.
  • How to evaluate cybersecurity threats using structured models.
  • How to develop recommendations for business continuity and disaster recovery.
